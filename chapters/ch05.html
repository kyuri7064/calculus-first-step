  3. 다항함수는 \((x^n)'=nx^{n-1}\)과 합·곱·몫의 미분법으로 바로 미분할 수 있어요.
  1. The function that sends each \(x\) to its derivative \(f'(x)\) is the derivative function.
  2. Finding it is called differentiating. Notation: \(f'(x),\ y',\ \frac{dy}{dx},\ \frac{d}{dx}f(x)\).
  3. Polynomials can be differentiated directly with \((x^n)'=nx^{n-1}\) and the sum, product and quotient rules.
도함수의 정의Definition $$f'(x)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$$ 미분 공식Rules $$(c)'=0,\quad (x^n)'=nx^{n-1},\quad (cf)'=cf',\quad (f\pm g)'=f'\pm g'$$ $$(fg)'=f'g+fg',\qquad \left(\frac fg\right)'=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}\ (g\ne0)$$

예 \((x^3-2x+5)'=3x^2-2\),   \(\left((x^2+1)(x-3)\right)'=2x(x-3)+(x^2+1)=3x^2-6x+1\). \(f(x)=|x|\)는 \(x=0\)에서 미분가능하지 않아서 도함수의 정의역에서 0이 빠져요.

e.g. \((x^3-2x+5)'=3x^2-2\),   \(\left((x^2+1)(x-3)\right)'=2x(x-3)+(x^2+1)=3x^2-6x+1\). \(|x|\) isn't differentiable at 0, so 0 is left out of the derivative's domain.
