  1. The number \(e\) is the limit of \(\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n\) (\(e\approx2.718\)); the log with base \(e\) is the natural log \(\ln\).

  3. The derivative of \(\ln x\) is \(\frac1x\); other bases bring in a factor of \(\ln a\).
핵심 극한Key limits $$e=\lim_{n\to\infty}\left(1+\frac1n\right)^n,\qquad \lim_{h\to0}\frac{e^h-1}{h}=1,\qquad \lim_{h\to0}\frac{\ln(1+h)}{h}=1$$ 도함수 (\(a>0,\ a\ne1\))Derivatives (\(a>0,\ a\ne1\)) $$(e^x)'=e^x,\quad (a^x)'=a^x\ln a,\quad (\ln x)'=\frac1x,\quad (\log_a x)'=\frac{1}{x\ln a},\quad (\ln|x|)'=\frac1x$$
